Safe Internet Practices

Browse carefully, check website addresses and use public Wi-Fi thoughtfully.

Stay One Step Ahead

Simple actions for safer digital habits.

01

Look for HTTPS

For sensitive activity, check that the site uses HTTPS and the domain is correct.

02

Avoid risky downloads

Do not install unknown files, extensions or pirated software.

03

Use public Wi-Fi carefully

Avoid sensitive activity on untrusted networks; prefer a trusted connection.

04

Check the website address

Fake sites can look almost identical to real ones.
